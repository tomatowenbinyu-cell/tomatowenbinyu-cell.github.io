## Research Interests

I am broadly interested in algebra, representation theory, algebraic combinatorics, and related interactions with geometry.

## Current and Previous Projects

### Character Theory of Finite Groups

Research project on Feit's conjectures and related arithmetic invariants in the character theory of finite groups.

### Lie Algebras in Verlinde Categories

Studying Lie algebras in the symmetric tensor category $\mathrm{Ver}_4$, with particular interest in classification problems in positive characteristic.

### Algebraic Combinatorics and Geometry

I have worked on problems involving Bott--Samelson varieties, random pipe dreams, Springer fibers, and related combinatorial and geometric structures.

### Farey Fractions and Equiaffine Geometry

Previous research on asymptotic questions arising from Farey fractions, Ford circles, and Hutchinson--Zaharescu parabolas.
